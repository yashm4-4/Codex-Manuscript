# Frozen proposal: typed target-evidence hierarchy

Apply at **candidate–gene–phenotype stratum–tissue** resolution. Preserve all seven evidence classes as separate columns and keep source, allele, genomic element/window and experimental unit. Assign the highest *supportable interpretation*, without creating a composite score. An unmapped source or conflicting gene remains unresolved. No tier says “causal gene.”

| Tier | Required independent evidence | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| A — mechanistic convergence | Exact candidate allele-specific endogenous perturbation with a reproducible target-expression/splicing effect in relevant cells **and** an independent regional disease–molecular shared-signal analysis or another distinct, replicated molecular link; full identity/phenotype gates pass | Strong candidate-to-gene mechanism, scoped to tested cells and phenotype; mediation/clinical causality still unproven |
| B — convergent locus/element support | Audited formal disease–QTL colocalization at gene/feature level **plus** independent region-to-gene evidence such as lung contact or endogenous region perturbation; or exact candidate eQTL plus an independent validated allele-specific target assay, with no unresolved contradiction | Strong gene/element hypothesis; identify whether evidence is locus or exact allele |
| C — suggestive mapping | Exact significant eQTL with or without harmonized risk-to-expression direction; published locus colocalization lacking exact candidate membership; physical contact alone; rE2G/ABC prediction; or separate published functional support at a linked variant | Suggestive and evidence-type labeled; no shared causal allele or mediation claim |
| Unresolved | Only TSS proximity, Catalog mapped gene, selected-locus membership, model score, motif prediction, broad Open Targets gene context, or an ambiguous/discordant join; or no usable evidence | No defensible target assignment; retain all competing genes and reasons |

Direct coding overlap is reported in its own coding-gene field, not promoted as a regulatory target. A published Moloc window can support a gene/QTL mechanism at **window scope**; it cannot make every V1 candidate in the window Tier B. A QTL plus rE2G link in overlapping GTEx/ENCODE data is informative but does not satisfy “independent” merely through two database labels. Record concordance and contradictions instead of voting by count. Prioritize COPD-relevant lung cell evidence over generic tissue only when assay quality and exact scope justify it; do not rewrite the V1 candidate rank.

Each table must report direct clinical/spirometric, EHR and ML/surrogate support separately, with A and C UKB overlap explicit. A molecular result may be shared across strata as the *same* QTL observation, but the associated GWAS phenotype and signed effect cannot be pooled. Where a candidate is ML-only in the frozen phenotype audit, a direct-COPD published Moloc result at a nearby locus stays a locus-level contextual note unless an exact valid association path exists.
