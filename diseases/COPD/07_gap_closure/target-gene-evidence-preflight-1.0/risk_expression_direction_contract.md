# Frozen risk-allele to expression-direction contract

Only a `PASS` from `eqtl_harmonization_contract.md` and a finite, nonzero frozen signed GWAS beta and GTEx v10 Lung ALT slope may produce a direction label. Convert the frozen disease-increasing allele to b38 REF or ALT. If it is ALT, `expression_change_per_risk_allele = slope`; if REF, use `-slope`. A positive result is increased normalized expression per disease-increasing allele; negative is decreased. Zero or nonfinite slopes remain unresolved. Use the source allele contrast, not a nominal association P value, to decide sign. Preserve effect magnitude, SE and uncertainty; a small/uncertain GWAS beta still has a signed contrast but must not be presented as confirmed candidate risk.

Report one row per **candidate × GWAS source row × eGene × tissue**. Do not collapse genes with opposite slopes, average phenotype tracks, or transfer an effect from a linked tag to an LD proxy. Require the GWAS signed row to be the *exact candidate allele* after cross-build harmonization. If only a linked tag has a GWAS effect, label it `TAG_ONLY_NO_CANDIDATE_DIRECTION`. Annotate A as direct ever-smoker COPD, B as direct Japanese clinical COPD, and C as secondary EHR J44; retain the frozen candidate phenotype-support classes separately. A/C cannot be counted as independent replication because of UKB overlap.

Permitted statement: “In [named GWAS] the disease-increasing allele at [exact allele] aligns with an increase/decrease in GTEx v10 bulk-lung normalized expression of [versioned Ensembl gene].” Also report the uncertainty, context and absence of colocalization. Prohibited statements: that the allele is causal, that expression mediates COPD, that the candidate regulates this gene in a particular cell, or that a QTL direction is transferable across ancestries/disease states. Sign concordance among QTLs is descriptive, not a colocalization test.

For future quality control, independently verify a small set of `same_alt`, `reversed_ref`, complement, indel and palindromic examples by manual allele algebra *before* any aggregate result; record mismatches and stop on a rule violation. This preflight freezes the design and performs no biological concordance tally.
