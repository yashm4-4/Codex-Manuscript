No target assignment, expression-direction result, or de novo colocalization is execution-cleared.** The frozen V1 list of 337 candidates, its order, all earlier V2 stages, and the manuscript are unchanged. The prior fine-mapping-input-resolution stage found A, B and C **NOT CLEARED** with zero execution packages; this report does not alter that finding.

## Answers for investigator review

1. **Current evidence.** V1's 4,779 candidate–gene rows are chiefly GENCODE v50 TSS proximity, GWAS Catalog tag-gene propagation, selected-locus membership, and 15 direct coding overlaps. GTEx v10 Lung supplies 915 exact significant variant–gene pairs at 185/337 candidates, spanning 224 eGenes. MPRAbase has one regional HepG2 sequence overlap, without an allele-pair assay; Open Targets has contextual COPD gene associations, not candidate-to-gene evidence. There is no V1 contact map, coloc, or endogenous allele perturbation. See `current_target_evidence_inventory.tsv`.
2. **Independence.** GTEx genotype–expression associations are a separate data modality from GWAS and sequence-model scores, but V1 GTEx eGene concordance with broad R006 proximity sets is not a second independent observation. Catalog tag mapping and selected-locus membership reuse GWAS labels; two nearby TSS rules reuse the same GENCODE annotation. Open Targets gene context may reuse GWAS/QTL literature and is not independent variant evidence. ENCODE rE2G and V1 ENCODE accessibility may share input lineage; ABC and rE2G from the same ENCODE record are one predictive family. Lung promoter capture Hi-C is a different physical-contact assay but is region/fragment level. Published Saferali Moloc reuses the Sakornsakolpat COPD GWAS and must not be counted as independent disease-association replication.
3. **Risk to expression.** Feasible in principle for exact, allele-resolved, same-build candidate matches where the frozen verified signed GWAS contrast can be joined to a GTEx ALT-effect slope. The GRCh37 A/B/C rows and GRCh38 GTEx rows require a unique, reference-checked cross-build identity; rsID or position alone is insufficient. No full candidate join or final concordance has been run. The permitted wording and all rejection states are frozen in `eqtl_harmonization_contract.md` and `risk_expression_direction_contract.md`.
4. **Formal colocalization.** None of the currently assembled GWAS × eQTL packages is EXECUTION-CLEARED. GTEx v10 significant pairs lack the full regional null and nonsignificant tests. A complete GRCh38 GTEx v8 Lung cis-eQTL source exists through the eQTL Catalogue, but its candidate locus slices, study N, overlap and QTL signal properties have not been assembled and audited here. A simple `coloc.abf` design needs dense regional SNP coverage and credible single-signal assumptions, though it does **not** require an LD matrix. Thus A's failure of SuSiE-RSS cannot by itself reject ABF. A remains INCONCLUSIVE for a future ABF pilot because exact cross-build coverage, regional precision failures, MAF/N parameterization and signal multiplicity have not been resolved. B's Japanese-versus-GTEx ancestry and SPA effect uncertainty, and C's SPA-derived SE/effective-N uncertainty, prevent present clearance. Multi-signal `coloc.susie` requires appropriate signed LD and compatible summary likelihoods; none of A/B/C passes those gates. Detailed method and pairwise decisions are in the two colocalization tables.
5. **Additional evidence worth acquiring.** Prioritize a small published Saferali lung/blood eQTL/sQTL/apaQTL Moloc crosswalk (especially lung splicing, with the paper's 33-versus-32 locus count discrepancy retained), ENCODE lung rE2G thresholded predictions, and, for unresolved intervals, Jung lung promoter capture Hi-C contacts. Use eQTL Catalogue GTEx v8 complete cis statistics only if a prespecified GWAS-defined locus/method gate passes. Cell-type eQTL files from Natri are a later, targeted sensitivity for a defined lung lineage; the normal/ILD cohort is not COPD. See `candidate_external_resource_inventory.tsv`.
6. **Next execution.** First make an allele-resolved candidate/GWAS/GTEx crosswalk and report eligible, ambiguous and absent denominators by frozen phenotype stratum, without interpreting LD proxies as candidate effects. Then import published Moloc results at their window/gene/QTL scope and add typed rE2G links; use lung contacts only where an explicit unresolved target question remains. A separate, newly approved colocalization stage may start only after a GWAS-defined locus and complete QTL regional data pass every applicable method gate. See `future_execution_specification.md`. Stop here for investigator review.

## Scope and scientific boundaries

The current frozen phenotype strata remain direct clinical/spirometric COPD, EHR COPD, and ML/surrogate liability. The phenotype audit found direct support for 184/337, EHR-only additional support for 29, and ML-only support for 124; these are *candidate-support* strata, not disease-risk effect estimates. A is an ever-smoker spirometric COPD GWAS, B is physician-diagnosed Japanese COPD, and C is UKB ICD-10 J44 EUR. A/C share UKB participants. A positive QTL or contact annotation cannot move a candidate to a different phenotype stratum or rerank the 337.

An exact eQTL pair reports an association between one variant genotype and expression. Even when the same allele is a signed COPD GWAS allele, two distinct causal variants in LD can produce both associations. Colocalization needs regional competing-variant evidence, adequate overlap, appropriate priors and an explicit single- or multiple-signal model; a shared signal remains a *locus-level* inference, not an exact candidate or mediation proof. Disease-risk-to-expression direction is an allele arithmetic statement and must be withheld for an unresolved cross-build or palindromic join.

The preflight's resource and method assessment uses official/public source pages and frozen local files, without bulk new downloads. URLs, access date, scope and local SHA256 receipts are recorded in `provenance/acquisition_inspection_receipts.tsv`. `provenance/validation.json` records structural and boundary checks. `provenance/freeze.json` and `provenance/artifact_checksums.tsv` bind this stage. No earlier frozen payload was edited.

Method/source anchors: the [coloc data vignette](https://rdrr.io/cran/coloc/f/inst/doc/a02_data.Rmd) distinguishes ABF's dense regional single-signal input from SuSiE's LD requirement; [eQTL Catalogue data access](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/eqtl/Data_access/) documents ALT effect and full cis summary access; the [published COPD Moloc study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12481885/) supplies the independent lung/blood QTL results and its inconsistent 33/32 locus wording; [ENCODE lung rE2G](https://www.encodeproject.org/annotations/ENCSR528UQX/) is predictive; [GSE86189](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE86189) is the contact source; and [GSE227136](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE227136) is normal/ILD lung cell-type QTL context.
