Proceed with a new versioned stage, e.g. external-benchmark-v2-1.0, for a one-time evaluation of the already-frozen V2-C models against the frozen COPD external functional benchmark.
This stage is evaluation only.
Do not retrain, retune, recalibrate, alter thresholds, change controls, change labels, choose seeds, redesign the model, or modify internal-training-1.0, internal-test-1.0, or COPD-V2-BENCH1.0.
The external benchmark must now be opened exactly once under the frozen V2 model.
1. Pre-open freeze
Before reading any benchmark outcome/label field, create and hash a prospective evaluation specification.
Prespecify:
- exact benchmark version and hashes;
- exact frozen V2-C checkpoint hashes;
- exact three-seed ensemble rule;
- exact symmetric forward/RC inference rule;
- frozen region thresholds:
  - enhancer 0.74848511815071117
  - H3K27me3-associated 0.76960810025533055
- benchmark inclusion/exclusion rules;
- exact variant-identity handling;
- exact REF/ALT orientation rules;
- exact metrics to be reported;
- directionality conventions;
- missing/unevaluable handling;
- no post-outcome tuning.
Preserve the original benchmark unchanged.
2. Evaluation populations
Use the existing frozen benchmark taxonomy.
Separately report:
- all exact benchmark identities;
- experimentally positive in-scope variants;
- exact V2-scorable positives;
- reporter-positive subsets;
- endogenous-editing-positive subsets;
- splice-only or other out-of-model mechanisms;
- context-specific null/positive observations;
- variants lacking exact sequence identity.
Do not convert missing, unresolved, context-mismatched, or out-of-model variants into negatives.
Do not use LD proxies.
Do not rescue identity approximately.
3. V2 sequence scoring
For every exact V2-scorable benchmark variant:
- construct the exact frozen 2,001-bp REF and ALT sequence inputs using the same allele-identity and indel geometry rules used previously;
- score REF and ALT independently in both nucleotide orientations;
- use the frozen three-seed symmetric C ensembles only.
For each model/context record:
- REF region score;
- ALT region score;
- delta = ALT - REF;
- absolute delta;
- sign;
- max(REF,ALT) region score;
- whether either allele passes the frozen region threshold.
No allele-delta threshold may be invented.
No region threshold may be changed.
4. Region-level benchmark evaluation
First ask only whether experimentally positive variants lie in regions the corrected V2-C models recognize as enhancer-associated or H3K27me3-associated.
Report for exact in-scope positive variants:
- enhancer region-threshold recovery;
- H3K27me3 region-threshold recovery;
- union recovery;
- continuous region-score distributions;
- rank/percentile summaries relative to an appropriate frozen internal reference distribution if such a reference was prespecified and can be used without outcome-driven construction.
Treat these as descriptive case-series recovery statistics unless a valid complete denominator exists.
Do not call them sensitivity if the denominator is incomplete.
5. Allelic-effect evaluation
Separately evaluate whether V2 REF–ALT deltas correspond to experimentally observed allelic direction.
Use only benchmark observations where:
- exact nucleotide identity is resolved;
- the experimental direction is unambiguous;
- REF/ALT mapping to the reported experimental alleles is unambiguous;
- the assayed mechanism is reasonably within the model’s intended regulatory scope.
For this direction-resolved subset report:
- sign concordance for enhancer delta;
- sign concordance for H3K27me3 delta where biologically interpretable;
- continuous delta values;
- per-locus results;
- exact numerator/denominator.
If the number of direction-resolved cases is small, state that explicitly and do not overinterpret a percentage.
Preserve splice-only cases such as NPNT as mechanistically out-of-model rather than false negatives.
6. Compare V1 and V2 descriptively
Since V1 benchmark results are already frozen, compare V2 against those existing results without modifying either stage.
Report:
- V1 forward region-call recovery;
- V1 RC region-call recovery;
- V2-C symmetric region-call recovery;
- variants newly recovered by V2;
- variants lost relative to V1;
- variants still missed;
- rs2013701 explicitly as a pre-existing known case, not a blinded validation case.
Do not perform significance testing that assumes independence between V1 and V2 when the same benchmark variants are reused.
This comparison is retrospective/descriptive.
7. Do not manufacture unavailable classification metrics
Retain the benchmark denominator rules from COPD-V2-BENCH1.0.
Unless a source has a complete, unbiased positive/negative denominator with all inclusion and QC outcomes available, do not calculate:
- sensitivity;
- specificity;
- FPR;
- AUROC;
- AUPRC;
- enrichment p-values based on incomplete negatives.
Do not treat Gong null assay-context rows as universal variant negatives.
Do not treat unidentified Castaldi hits as null.
8. Context and mechanism stratification
Where support permits, report results separately by:
- source study;
- cell type;
- assay type;
- reporter vs endogenous perturbation;
- enhancer-like vs repressor/H3K27me3-relevant mechanism;
- SNV vs indel;
- exact V1-presence vs newly scorable V2 sequence.
Keep small strata descriptive.
9. Hard firewall after opening
Once benchmark outcomes are opened:
- no retraining;
- no threshold change;
- no seed exclusion;
- no checkpoint replacement;
- no new control construction;
- no architecture change;
- no “one more” calibration;
- no returning to internal-training-1.0 or internal-test-1.0.
If V2 performs poorly, report the failure honestly and stop.
Any future redesign must be a separately versioned scientific project and must not claim the same benchmark as untouched validation.
10. Required outputs
Produce:
- pre-open prospective specification and hash;
- benchmark source/hash verification;
- exact benchmark-to-V2 identity map;
- sequence-construction QC;
- per-variant REF/ALT V2-C scores;
- region-level recovery table;
- direction-resolved allelic-effect table;
- V1-vs-V2 descriptive comparison;
- source/mechanism stratified summaries;
- explicit unevaluable/missing table;
- limitation statement;
- independent validation;
- append-only activity/decision/result register updates;
- final checksum/freeze record.
11. Stop
At completion report:
- how many experimentally positive benchmark variants were exact and V2-scorable;
- how many were recovered by enhancer, H3K27me3 and their union;
- how this compares with frozen V1;
- direction concordance among the strictly direction-resolved subset;
- whether the corrected model shows improved external functional recovery;
- whether any apparent improvement is region-level only or extends to REF–ALT effects;
- the major remaining failure modes.
Then stop.
Do not score the broader COPD GWAS candidate universe yet.
Do not rerank the 337 V1 candidates.
Do not perform fine-mapping or target-gene analysis.
