# Unsent data-request draft: Sakornsakolpat 2019 / GCST007692

Status: DRAFT ONLY. No recipient contacted and no request sent. Investigator authorization is required before sending.

Subject: Request for complete signed COPD GWAS summary statistics and compatible LD information

Dear study investigators,

We are preparing a COPD regulatory-genomics study and would like to evaluate whether the Sakornsakolpat 2019 COPD GWAS can support a defensible statistical fine-mapping analysis. Could you advise on access to the complete signed genome-wide discovery/meta-analysis summary statistics, including nonsignificant variants, and any ancestry-specific or cohort-specific releases?

For each intended release, we would need the phenotype and case/control definitions; cohort composition and overlap; ancestry strata; nominal and per-variant case/control/sample counts; genome assembly and coordinate convention; chromosome, position, reference/alternate and effect/other alleles; signed beta or odds ratio; the effect scale and standard-error definition; association-test P values and test implementation; allele frequencies by group where available; imputation and variant-QC fields; duplicate/multiallelic handling; and release checksums and provenance. Please distinguish discovery statistics from selected follow-up or replication results.

We also seek guidance on dense signed LD or sufficient-statistic covariance compatible with the analysis: contributing samples and ancestry, genotype/dosage and imputation release, covariate treatment, counted alleles and variant index, LD sample size, and an appropriate treatment of meta-analysis cohort/ancestry weights and overlap. If matrices cannot be distributed publicly, please identify the legitimate application route and relevant use conditions. An ancestry-specific source with a compatible LD contract would be particularly useful.

The public 19,373-record dbGaP display export uses absolute coefficients and is not a complete signed release; we will not reconstruct the analysis or infer missing signs from it. We would respect applicable access conditions and discuss collaboration and attribution as appropriate.

Thank you for your guidance.
