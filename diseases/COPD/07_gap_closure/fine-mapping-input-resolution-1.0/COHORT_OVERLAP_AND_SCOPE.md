# Cohort and analysis scope

Track A is GCST90016588, the European UK Biobank ever-smoker spirometric COPD case-control analysis (12,446 cases, 59,145 controls). Track B is GCST90013709, the original combined-sex Japanese physician-diagnosed COPD analysis (3,315 cases, 201,592 controls), including BBJ and the documented ToMMo/IMM/JPHC/J-MICC control contributions. Track C is Pan-UKB ICD10 J44 EUR (11,536 cases, 408,995 controls), an EHR/ICD secondary sensitivity.

Tracks A and C reuse UK Biobank participants and must not be treated as independent replications. Their ascertainment, subgroup selection, association implementation and genotype/sample releases differ. The Pan-UKB EUR LD index has 420,542 samples, whereas J44 has 420,531 phenotype-level observations; equal project names do not establish identical samples or covariance. That LD is an external full-European UKB reference for Kim's smoking-selected analysis. No pooled A/C estimate, combined P value, averaged posterior or independent-replication count is constructed here.

Track B is a distinct Japanese ancestry track with clinical ascertainment. Its pooled cohort/control contribution must be accounted for by any future Japanese LD source; "Japanese" or "BBJ" labels alone do not establish that contract. Sex-specific or sex-meta analyses were not analyzed. Their presence in the original downloaded archive is a packaging dependency, and archive integrity validation does not constitute an additional scientific analysis.

Only the three authorized GWAS tracks define loci. Other ancestry columns in Pan-UKB's complete flat-file release and other phenotype records in its small column-metadata container are acquisition dependencies; only J44 EUR statistics and metadata enter scientific outputs. The provider variant manifest is used for variant identity and QC, not its gene annotations. GENCODE's GRCh37.p13 reference is used as a sequence reference, not as gene or regulatory evidence.

No V1 candidate list, V2 model output, functional benchmark, gene preference, expression QTL or regulatory annotation influences locus definition or input eligibility. There is no model inference, posterior calculation, credible set, colocalization, target-gene assignment or manuscript change. The Sakornsakolpat request remains an unsent draft and its unsuitable unsigned public export is not reconstructed.
