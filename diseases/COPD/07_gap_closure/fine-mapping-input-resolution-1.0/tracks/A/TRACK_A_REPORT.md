## Signed statistic and source transformations

The [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8096488/) describes smoking-stratum marginal logistic GWAS using PLINK 2.0 and marker filters MAF≥0.01 and imputation r²≥0.5. The native accession labels effect and other alleles. [PLINK's format contract](https://www.cog-genomics.org/plink/2.0/formats#glm_logistic) defines OR for the tested allele and SE for log-odds. Therefore the tested transformation is `beta=log(OR)` and `z=beta/SE`, with two-sided Wald P. Exact historical commands and author-side formatting scripts remain unavailable; the audit does not claim to have inspected them.

The numerical gate was saved before acquisition and before whole-file evaluation. It propagates the literal printed decimal precision of OR, SE and P through the normal-tail calculation, with `1e-10` log-P numerical slack. **9,861,764 rows pass; 25,089 fail and remain quarantined.** None of the failed rows has P<5×10⁻⁸. The deterministic supplementary sample spans chromosome/P bands and contains 22,495 records. All significant rows and all 11 lead variants pass. Some failure strings display binary-float serialization tails such as `1.0098800000000001`; the separate formatted source preserves the same strings and does not resolve their original numerical precision. The frozen gate was not loosened and no OR, SE, P or allele was changed to improve consistency.

Within the complete harmonized release, all **9,594,025 orientable records** pass the frozen reciprocal-OR/log-OR sign and allele-transform checks (`1e-12` tolerances); 94,994 records remain unoriented. Exact original-field provenance keys link 9,344,885 harmonized records to native rows; 344,134 harmonized keys are unmatched and remain explicit. Catalog updates identifiers and coordinates, but an unproven exact crosswalk is not invented. These GRCh38 results corroborate transformation semantics; they never replace GRCh37 native locus coordinates. See `results/harmonized_audit.json` and the full retained sources.
