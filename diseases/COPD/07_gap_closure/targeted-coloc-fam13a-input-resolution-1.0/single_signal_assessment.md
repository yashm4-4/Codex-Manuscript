# Prespecified single-signal assessment

**Classification: INCONCLUSIVE.** This judgment uses the whole frozen GWAS locus and the complete FAM13A QTL slice, without reference to the known candidate's association or editing result.

Track A has 51 genome-wide significant rows in the 3.26 Mb native locus: 50 lie in the native 89.8 Mb 100-kb bin, one in the 90.0 Mb bin. The lead is an indel at 89,834,173 (P = 2.25222×10⁻¹⁰) and lacks an eligible cross-build row. Spatial clustering alone cannot establish one conditional signal, and the separated hit cannot establish a second one. There is no verified conditional-GWAS decomposition for this exact phenotype/locus in the frozen inputs. Existing LD diagnostics belong to a separate, not-cleared fine-mapping package and are not interpreted as conditional evidence here.

The eQTL Catalogue FAM13A slice has 7,010 tested variants, best nominal P ≈ 5.6×10⁻⁴, adjusted permutation P ≈ 0.605, and zero official FAM13A credible sets. Thus the official source reports no independent FAM13A eQTL signal to count. Two distant suggestive nominal peaks do not justify asserting either one or multiple independent QTL signals without conditional evidence.

[coloc.abf](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/articles/vignette.html) assumes at most one causal variant per trait in the analyzed region. The assumption is **not demonstrated** for GWAS and is **not assessable as a detected QTL signal** in this v8 release. A strong ABF PP.H4 from an incomplete/multiple-signal region would be misleading. No vanilla ABF is authorized from this stage.
