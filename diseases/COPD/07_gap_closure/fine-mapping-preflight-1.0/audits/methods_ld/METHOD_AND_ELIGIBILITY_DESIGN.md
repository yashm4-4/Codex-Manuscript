# Fine-mapping method and eligibility design

Stage: `fine-mapping-preflight-1.0`; public-documentation/metadata audit only. No LD matrix values, locus computations, model scoring, candidate intersections or fine-mapping were performed. **Accessible documentation/header metadata is not whole-file validation; whole-file validation is not locus-level inference readiness.** Expected eligible locus count and actual runtime are not determined here.

## Recommendation and status ladder

Use multi-signal, summary-statistic **SuSiE-RSS** as the prospective primary method only after the signed association/LD contract and locus QC are satisfied. Use **FINEMAP** as a prespecified same-input sensitivity analysis, not a vote that repairs unsuitable LD. Keep variant priors uniform; no V2, functional-benchmark, eQTL, gene or candidate-derived priors or locus selection. An accessible weaker phenotype must not replace the direct COPD anchor in interpretation. The strongest primary phenotype can remain blocked while a separately labeled EHR phenotype becomes technically feasible.

Distinguish: `PUBLIC_SOURCE_IDENTIFIED` → `HEADER/SCHEMA_ACCESS_VERIFIED` → `FULL_INPUT_VALIDATED` → `LD_CONTRACT_VALIDATED` → `LOCUS_QC_PASSED` → `AUTHORIZED_FOR_INFERENCE`. This preflight establishes only the first two where documented; none of the later stages is implied. A signed matrix plus matching ancestry label is insufficient: cohort composition, phenotype sample subset, imputation, statistic, covariates, effective sample sizes and per-variant missingness matter. [SuSiE-RSS primary paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1010299), [meta-analysis consistency study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839193/)

## Locus selection: prospective, phenotype-specific, candidate-blind

1. After complete source validation, choose association loci solely from that GWAS's own valid, source-QC-passing association test with **P < 5×10⁻⁸**. This is a conventional selection rule, not a newly calculated study result. Preserve the original P encoding and treat zero/underflow correctly. Do not choose a lead from existing COPD candidates, functional benchmarks, V2 outputs, known genes or desired biological stories.
2. Proposed initial intervals are 3 Mb total (±1.5 Mb around association leads), clipped at native chromosome limits and merged transitively when overlapping. Freeze deterministic lead tie ordering (association significance, native chromosome/position, exact allele key, source row ID). Retain all eligible dense variant statistics in each interval, including nonsignificant measurements. Lead association significance is not a per-variant fine-mapping inclusion filter.
3. Treat this interval convention as a starting design with published precedent, not proof that all relevant LD fits within it. Before inference, inspect association/LD boundary adequacy under a frozen rule. If long-range LD, many signals, structural variation or large merged regions exceed the validated design, flag/defer for a separately frozen complex-locus plan. Do not shrink a locus, split a correlated signal, or drop variants merely to fit memory.
4. Exclude MHC from the first standard pass. FinnGen's documented mask is chr6:25–34 Mb **GRCh38 inclusive**; a broad BBJ/UKBB fine-mapping precedent uses chr6:25–36 Mb. The proposal is a deliberately conservative chr6:25–36 Mb operational exclusion in each native GRCh37/GRCh38 assembly, **not a claimed exact liftover equivalence or biological MHC boundary**. Freeze assembly/coordinate convention and the conservative rationale before execution; exclude the entire merged interval if it intersects the mask rather than splitting away correlated portions. Complex HLA-specific methods require a separate authorization.
5. Use autosomes in the initial contract; sex chromosomes require a separate dosage/ploidy, sample and test specification. Overlapping intervals within one study become one joint locus. Reused cohorts across studies do not become independent replication or independent fine-mapping signals.

The association threshold and interval scale have [FinnGen pipeline precedent](https://finngen.gitbook.io/documentation/methods/finemapping), and the wider conservative MHC interval has [BBJ/FinnGen/UKBB methods precedent](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.09.03.21262975v1.full). The proposed same-number mask across builds is explicitly a design choice, not a source-derived coordinate conversion. **No loci were selected or counted here.**

## QC gates that must be done later

- **Identity/density:** full file completeness and duplicates; exact reference/allele/build alignment; same row/column ordering; all retained variants present in signed LD; attrition counts by reason, allele frequency, source QC and association strength. Report missing lead/strong variants as a locus blocker. No fabricated “90% overlap” or other universal coverage threshold. Do not impute missing statistics or substitute LD proxies silently.
- **LD integrity:** symmetry after documented triangular reconstruction, positive diagonals, correlation scaling/range, positive semidefiniteness/eigenspectrum, rank/condition and long-range/banding effects, exact LD sample N and coverage. Numerical tolerances must be fixed against the actual format/precision and software before execution. No silent ridge, diagonal reset, eigenvalue clipping, near-PD repair, sign guessing or band-zero filling. Valid deterministic representation conversion is distinct from scientific LD repair and must be logged.
- **Statistic/sample compatibility:** source effect/test and P/Z relationship, nominal versus effective N, per-variant sample-size variation, imputation and allele-frequency differences, mixed-model/covariate correction, ancestry and phenotype sample subset. Record `N_cases`, `N_controls`, nominal N and any method-specific effective N separately; do not automatically replace n by `4/(1/N_cases + 1/N_controls)` without an approved likelihood/statistic derivation.
- **Summary/LD consistency:** future `estimate_s_rss`, conditional/kriging residual plots, and documented diagnostic flags; optionally SLALOM/DENTIST-derived checks with their assumptions explicit. A small inconsistency estimate is not proof of matched LD. The SuSiE vignette's example lambda is not a universal cutoff. Quarantine unresolved inconsistent loci instead of increasing regularization until desired signals appear.
- **Cohort overlap:** keep study/cohort and participant-overlap ledger, repeated releases/sex strata/case definitions and reused controls. Do not combine dependent outputs by Fisher/Stouffer, average PIPs across dependent studies, count them as replication, or pool meta-analysis statistics against a convenient population matrix. Overlap corrections need an explicit approved model.
- **Future fit diagnostics:** convergence, evidence-bound/search trace, sensitivity to signal cap/initialization/priors, CS purity and boundary behavior, and consistency between appropriately matched primary/sensitivity methods. Method agreement does not cure shared input error. Report inconclusive/low-purity/unstable sets rather than discarding inconvenient results.

No numerical readiness threshold is supplied where these sources do not justify a transportable cutoff. A future analysis may freeze a principled implementation-specific threshold with justification before opening its inference outputs, but this preflight must not retroactively label such QC as passed.

## Prospective model configuration, not executed

Propose a primary maximum of **10 single effects / causal variants per locus**, uniform priors and nominal **95% credible-set coverage**, matching published multi-biobank practice; this cap is a working capacity, not an assertion that ten causes exist or that every locus is adequately represented. A separately specified same-input cap sensitivity (e.g. L=5 and L=20) would evaluate saturation; it must be authorized and frozen before any inference. If a locus saturates the cap or is unstable, report it as unresolved rather than choosing the configuration that favors desired variants.

SuSiE's documented `min_abs_corr=0.5` is a credible-set **minimum absolute Pearson r** reporting filter (r²=0.25), not an LD-reference eligibility threshold. Nominal 95% coverage is conditional on a valid model and data; it does not establish frequentist coverage under LD mismatch or prove biological causality. Residual-variance estimation is off for reference LD; enabling it requires genuinely matching original data/statistics, not just the same biobank name. [SuSiE-RSS reference](https://stephenslab.github.io/susieR/reference/susie_rss.html)

Archived current documentation identifies susieR 0.16.6 and includes newer finite-reference/mismatch options. No installed-version assumption is made. Before execution, pin package version/source hash, R/BLAS and all algorithmic options, iteration limits/tolerances, refinement behavior and random seeds. Do not silently adopt new LD-mismatch correction or empirical-Bayes priors. FINEMAP's official site currently lists 1.4.2; pin the actual binary, license and options, including maximum causal variants, stochastic-search settings and repeated seeded runs. Its default maximum of five is not the proposed cap of ten. Documentation was retrieved over HTTP only after HTTPS certificate failures and is marked accordingly in provenance. [FINEMAP official specification](http://www.christianbenner.com/)

## Compute and storage design

No benchmark or fine-mapping runtime was measured. Plan CPU jobs only; no GPU training or inference is relevant. Full PanUKB LD is documented as 43.3 TB (EUR 14.1 TB); copying it is unnecessary and not authorized. Retrieve only approved locus blocks and the exact variant index under a later data-access plan, preserving source/index/object hashes and reconstruction steps. Sparse triangular storage requires correct reconstruction and explicit distance-band handling, not treating unavailable entries as independently validated zero correlations. [PanUKB storage documentation](https://pan.ukbb.broadinstitute.org/docs/hail-format/index.html)

Engineering memory estimates, not observed performance: a dense float64 p×p matrix occupies `8 p²` bytes: p=5,000 → 0.20 GB; 10,000 → 0.80 GB; 20,000 → 3.20 GB; 50,000 → 20.0 GB (decimal). Budget multiple working copies plus eigensystem/factorization, index and fit state; a provisional 3–6 matrix-copy allowance is planning headroom, not a method guarantee. Iterative matrix products scale roughly with Lp²; dense eigendecomposition scales cubically in p. Concurrency must follow measured future memory rather than total CPU count. Large/complex loci are deferred, never silently truncated. Download duration, cloud request/egress costs, per-locus runtime and total eligible loci remain unknown until authorized full input/LD QC.

Preserve original summary statistics and LD source pointers/checksums, harmonized local inputs and exact variant order, all attrition/QC tables, configuration/environment, every attempted fit/log, CS/PIP outputs and failure reasons. Reference LD panels must scale appropriately with study size; [Benner et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5630179/) show why a small 1000 Genomes population subset cannot be assumed adequate for a large biobank merely because its ancestry label matches.

## Evidence and audit limits

`methods_ld_audit.json` binds every exact downloaded source/error body to URL, UTC, HTTP/transport status, size and SHA-256, and separates bounded prefixes from complete responses. Source bytes include documentation, source code, matrix/index **metadata**, and the 1KG sample manifest, not LD matrix values. HTTP 200 is not a semantic pass: redirect stubs and transport/access errors are retained explicitly. The BBJ methods preprint was readable through the web research index, but direct HTML/PDF archival probes returned HTTP 403; these error bodies are not represented as archived full articles. The proposed L=10 cap is also directly documented in the successfully archived FinnGen methods, and the wider MHC mask remains an explicitly conservative design choice. Benner's reference-size paper was successfully archived from PMC after other endpoints failed. Public mutable source code was inspected, not imported or executed. All scientific locus QC remains `NOT_RUN_PREFLIGHT_ONLY`; no old stage, checkpoint, candidate or benchmark file was opened by this subtask.
